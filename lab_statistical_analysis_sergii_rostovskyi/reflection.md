# Reflection

I expected the campaign with the lowest CPA to lead across most metrics. Campaign 30801593 did have the lowest aggregate CPA, but campaign 10341182 brought in more than twice as many conversions and campaign 15184511 converted a higher share of clicks. The ranking changed with the business objective. That mattered more than most of the individual p-values.

Multiple-comparison correction affected one CPA decision. The difference between campaigns 15398570 and 29427842 passed BH but fell short of Bonferroni. A separate result showed that even a very small adjusted p-value can come with a conversion-rate difference below the threshold for a meaningful budget change.

The study is limited by transformed costs, the missing revenue field, one platform, a short observation window, and correlated exposures. I would show stakeholders the cost, conversion rate, volume, and confidence intervals side by side. The decision I would bring to the CMO is how much to put into a controlled pilot, what outcome would justify expanding it, and what evidence is still missing.
