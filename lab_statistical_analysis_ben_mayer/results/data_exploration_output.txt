platform-days have 0 or 1 ads and CPA is undefined or one ad's luck. Weekly sums give
~5 ads per cell and ~155 complete weeks per platform. Partial first/last weeks are dropped.

==============================================================================
KEY INSIGHTS
==============================================================================
- Best CPA: Google Search ($5.31); worst: LinkedIn ($431.56).
- Best ROAS: Google Search (16.73x). ROAS < 1 (loses money): LinkedIn.
- Most variable weekly CPA (CV): LinkedIn (0.53).
- Meta, Snapchat and TikTok sit in a tight $25-31 CPA cluster: that is where the tests matter.
- Mean of weekly CPAs is above the pooled CPA (e.g. Google Search $6.43 vs $5.31): averaging
  ratios over-weights low-volume weeks. Tests use weekly values; the budget view uses pooled totals.
- Outliers are single big-revenue weeks (right tail), not data errors; kept.
