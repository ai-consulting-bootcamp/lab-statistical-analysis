==============================================================================
KEY ANSWERS
==============================================================================
- Enough data? For the full panel, yes: every significant pair has ~100% power at 154 weeks.
- Under the spec's 15% noise, 13 weeks detects only large gaps; a 10% CPA gap needs 37 weeks.
- Under real noise (CV 0.4), a 10% gap needs 261 weeks and a 5% gap 1021 weeks per channel; even the full 3 years cannot resolve 5%.
- The spec's 15% noise assumption understates required sample size by ~(CV_obs/0.15)^2 = 7.1x. Plan tests on measured variance, not a textbook default.
- Pairs a 13-week review would likely miss: Meta vs TikTok (21% gap, 28% power, needs 49 wk); Meta vs Snapchat (28% gap, 41% power, needs 35 wk).
