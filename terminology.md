--- MARKETING METRICS ---

Impressions
How many times the ad was shown.
Used to measure reach and to calculate CTR.

Clicks
How many times users clicked the ad.
Used to measure engagement and as the denominator for conversion rate.

Spent
How much money was spent on the ad.
Used to evaluate efficiency and calculate CPA.

Total_Conversion
All conversions attributed to the ad.
A broader conversion measure from the source dataset.

Approved_Conversion
Conversions that were actually approved/validated.
This is the stricter outcome and the one we will mainly use for CPA and conversion-rate analysis.

---

Clicks = clicks on the ad.
Total_Conversion = people who enquired about the product after seeing the ad.
Approved_Conversion = people who bought the product after seeing the ad.

**CTR** = Clicks / Impressions
How efficiently impressions generate clicks.
how effectively an ad gets attention and generates clicks. Higher is generally better.

Approved Conversion Rate = Approved_Conversion / Clicks
How efficiently clicks generate approved conversions.
Higher is better. - 200% , approved conversation couns all purchases - so Approved Conversion Rate is not usefull

**Purchase Rate** = Approved_Conversion / Total_Conversion

**CPA** = Spent / Approved_Conversion
How much it costs to generate one approved conversion.
Lower is better.

---

CTR
→ Does the ad attract clicks?

Purchase Rate
→ Of the recorded conversions/enquiries, how many become approved conversions/purchases?

CPA
→ How much did an approved conversion/purchase cost?
